About Ross

I am currently a software engineer based in Dundee, Scotland. I build games, tools, & software to solve problems for a broad range of fields & industries.

I’ve spent the majority of my career bridging the gap between many game development disciplines, often by building tools & processes to get the work done that little bit easier. I can identify solutions to problems at the lower levels of the development pipeline, or general high level across the team.

I see myself as a bit of a generalist, as I am comfortable wearing multiple hats & switching contexts quickly between engineering, production pipelines, & design/qa/art where I’m needed. My entire career has spanned from QA testing, tools development, game development, backend & frontend web development, as well as dev-ops & build/deployment pipeline engineering.

I currently work with Lloyds Banking group developing games & gamified experiences around financial topics with Scottish Widows. I’ve been part of a small, fast-moving team from their inception, helping define the game development processes & pipelines in a large organisation that have never built games. I focus on enabling the team to work effectively with modern game development tools & pipelines while navigating the regulated financial development environments already established in the bank.
